# ATMA Deep Dive

**Open it here: https://rsk1976.github.io/ATMA_DEEPDIVE/**

An interactive walk-through of how ATMA (Adaptive Technology for Motorized Applications) designs an electric vehicle powertrain.
It starts from a customer's few words (type, place, budget, one need) and follows every step to the result: the vehicle requirement, the Bengaluru weather and roads, the hardest real trips, the law's test cycle, the sizing of the motor, battery and charger, the parts chosen from a catalogue, the customer spec sheet, the powertrain simulation and the bill of materials.

Every block shows where its data comes from, how each number is calculated (formulas with the real values) and where it goes next. The example is a three-wheeler (Trike N, L1) for two customers, Murali and Sunita.
